# Changelog

## 0.4.1 (2026-11-11)
- The menu bar icon now looks like the app icon: a proper macaron.
- First version shared outside our own Macs: download it or install it with Homebrew.

## 0.4.0 (2026-11-08)
- Turn several photos into one animated GIF that loops.
- Videos: make an MP4, a small GIF of the first seconds, a smaller 1080p or 720p copy, or keep
  only the sound as M4A. A percentage shows while it works, and Cancel stops it.
- Sound files: switch between M4A, WAV and AIFF.
- Word, RTF and HTML documents to PDF or plain text, and the text of a PDF to Word (layout and
  pictures stay behind, and the panel says so).
- Right-click videos, sound files and documents in Finder too: Convert with Macarum.

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-07)
- Convert files from the menu bar: drop photos, SVGs or PDFs on the panel (or choose them), pick
  what to make and press Convert: JPG, PNG or one PDF, a photo under 5 MB, 2 MB, 1 MB or 500 KB for
  an upload form, or PDF pages as images.
- Right-click images or PDFs in Finder and choose Convert with Macarum: the panel opens with them
  ready. Results land next to the original or in a folder you pick, and can go straight to the
  clipboard.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-07)
- Select several clipboard items with ⌘-click, ⇧-click or ⇧↑/⇧↓, then pin or delete them together, also in search results.
- Delete selected items with ⌘⌫; ⌫ alone only ever edits the search text.
- Changed your mind? Undo a delete for 5 seconds with the Undo button or ⌘Z.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-06)
- Clipboard history: text, links, colours, images and files, searchable, kept 1 week by default (up to 30 days).
- Open it by pressing ⌘ three times, or pick a keyboard shortcut instead (it refuses clashes).
- Paste, paste as plain text, copy, edit or delete straight from the panel; clear all history.
- Pin items to keep them: they never expire and Clear All leaves them alone.
- Copies from password managers and copies marked private are never saved.
- Memory: click the menu bar icon to see memory pressure, swap and the apps using the most
  memory, relaunch or quit them, or free cached memory. A dot on the icon warns when memory runs low.
- Macaron app and menu bar icons.
- Memory rows say "Idle 3 h" when an app or program has done no real work for an hour or more.
  It is only a label; nothing closes on its own.
